# artist-statement-tool
Figure out the who, what, and why of your artist practice with this question sequence for Claude or ChatGPT.

This tool is an automated interview, one question at a time, aimed at finding the resonating through line underneath the artist's works, resulting in a properly drafted artist statement for grant application use.
- This guide was built using the 2027 Guggenheim Fellowship application as a reference alongside deep research into what makes career narratives and grant statements actually persuasive.
- This guide is meant as an assistant to point you in the right direction. Adjust the final output responses with your own language and tone. 
- Technology is an assistant and cannot replace your human touch. Authenticity is key to drafting a successful statement.

Upload the file to a new chat and say "walk me through this." Use a free account, either Claude or ChatGPT. Answer honestly, one stage at a time, and it drafts once the throughline, career story and plans are on the page.
